## Avatar: `kongzoomaid-cat-seacret/` and `avatar.glb`

- **Model:** "Kongzoomaid Cat BW2" (texture variant "Seacret")

`kongzoomaid-cat-seacret/` is the original download, unchanged. `avatar.glb`
is our conversion: FBX to glTF binary with `tools/convert_avatar.py` (run in
Blender). The colour textures were re-linked, and the roughness and metallic
maps were replaced by constant values. Rig and skin weights are unchanged.
